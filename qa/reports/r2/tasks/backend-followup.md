Two follow-ups from this round.

1. The common-password check barely works: `src/common-passwords.ts` holds the 100 most common passwords, but only 2 of them are 10 characters or longer, and every password under 10 is already refused by length. Add the common passwords that ARE 10+ characters long (from public breach lists, e.g. password123, password1234, 1q2w3e4r5t, 1qaz2wsx3edc, qwerty123456, iloveyou123, administrator, welcome123, changeme123 and similar; 50-150 entries, lowercase), and also refuse a password made of one repeated character (aaaaaaaaaa) or a straight run of keyboard/number order (1234567890, 0987654321, abcdefghij, qwertyuiop, asdfghjkl;). Thai message as the other rules. Tests for each kind.
2. A real account may be named `guest` (USERNAME_RE allows it). Uploads of visitors use the owner name 'guest' (`user?.username ?? 'guest'` in `src/routes/upload.ts`), so such an account would share uploads (and the visitors' quota bookkeeping) with every visitor. Make `guest` (any case) a reserved name: creating such a user is refused. Do not rename existing accounts. Test it.
